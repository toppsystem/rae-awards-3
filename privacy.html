Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your email address. When you vote we store which nominee you chose in each category. When you buy a ticket we store your name, phone number, email and the Paystack payment reference.

Why

To verify that each person votes once per category, to issue and check your ticket at the door, and to contact you about your purchase or the event.

Who processes it

Google (sign-in), Supabase (database), Cloudflare (hosting and bot protection) and Paystack (payments). Card details are handled only by Paystack and never reach us.

Who can see it

Your votes are private and results are published only in totals. Your ticket details are visible only to the organisers.

Keeping and deleting

We keep this data for the RAE Awards 3.0 programme and a reasonable period after it. To ask us to access, correct or delete your data, message the organisers on WhatsApp: +234 810 347 4454.

Your rights

You have rights over your personal data under Nigerian data protection law, including access, correction and deletion where applicable.
