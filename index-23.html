Quick answers

How does voting work?

Sign in with Google, pick a nominee in each category and lock your vote. You get one vote per category, and votes are final.

Where is my ticket?

Sign in with the same Google account you bought with. Your tickets appear in Profile automatically. If you paid with a different email, use "Paid but no pass?" in Profile.

Why is the price a little higher at checkout?

Paystack adds a small processing fee to card payments. The ticket price itself does not change.

Is my vote private?

Yes. Nominees and the public never see who voted for whom.
